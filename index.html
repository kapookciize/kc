Who are Kapook and Ciize?

Kapook and Ciize are Thai actresses from GMMTV who became a fan-favourite pair as the side couple in the GL series Pluto (2024). They have since appeared together in the ensemble series Girl Rules, and fans have followed them through fanmeets, interviews and shows. This archive keeps those moments in one place.
